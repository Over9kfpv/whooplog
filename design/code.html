// BENCH EXECUTION PROTOCOL SHRED SQUAD HOW-TO
STEP 01

Direct Phase Soldering & Scraping Solder Mask

Micro motor plugs (Pico 1.25) add 0.42g per corner and create high-impedance voltage drop spikes during snap-throttle maneuvers. Desoldered the 3-pin factory sockets directly off the Diamond F4 AIO board. Using a precision chisel tip at 365°C and Kester 951 liquid flux, the 34AWG copper strands were pre-tinned and tack-soldered flush with the AIO perimeter copper pours.

check_circle RESISTANCE TEST: 0.08Ω Phase-to-Phase across all 4 quadrants (PASS)
STEP 02

Bluejay 48kHz ESC Flash & Dynamic D-Shot RPM Filtering

Stock BLHeli_S 24kHz PWM results in severe motor braking stutter and poor battery life on sub-20g frames. Using ESC-Configurator via WebSerial over Google Chrome, flashed the BB21 ESC target with Bluejay 0.19.2 O-H-5 48kHz. This shifted the motor frequency harmonic out of the gyro sampling spectrum, permitting much lower D-term filtering in Betaflight.

speed THROTTLE RESPONSE: +18% flight time improvement (3m 30s -> 4m 12s)
STEP 03

Frame Weight-Loss Chop & Canopy Reinforcement

Took a scalpel blade to the plastic battery cradle, substituting it with a custom 0.3mm braided Kevlar friction loop. Snipped extraneous cross-braces on the ducted motor arches, then applied a micro-bead of E6000 flexible adhesive along the camera mount pivot to prevent micro-jellovision vibration transfer to the SPI gyro.

lightbulb // BENCH_PRO_TIP: ULTRASONIC FLUX PURGE

Rosins used in leaded 63/37 solder can slowly absorb airborne moisture over months, causing micro-capacitance leakage between the BMI270 gyro SPI clock lines. Dilute your leftover solder rosin in 99% anhydrous isopropyl alcohol, scrub lightly with a trimmed boar-bristle toothbrush, and bake the board at 60°C for 10 minutes on your 3D printer heatbed before maiden flight.
